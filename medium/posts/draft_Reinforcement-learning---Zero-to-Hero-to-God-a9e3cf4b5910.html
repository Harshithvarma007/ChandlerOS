Phase 1: The Mathematical Bedrock (Zero)

Focus: Understanding the Agent-Environment loop without the noise of Deep Learning.

Blog Topics (Theory):

  1. The RL Framework: Agents, Environments, State ($S$), Action ($A$), Reward ($R$).
  2. Markov Decision Processes (MDPs): The Markov Property, Trajectories, and Discount Factors ($\gamma$).
  3. The Bellman Equations: The heart of RL. Deriving $V(s)$ (Value Function) and $Q(s, a)$ (Action-Value Function).
  4. Dynamic Programming: Policy Iteration vs. Value Iteration.

Kaggle Notebooks (Practice):

  • Notebook 1: Solving FrozenLake from Scratch. Implement the Bellman updates manually using Python dictionaries (no Neural Networks yet).
  • Notebook 2: Monte Carlo vs. Temporal Difference (TD). Implementing Q-Learning and SARSA on Taxi-v3.

Phase 2: Value-Based Deep RL (The Bridge)

Focus: Replacing “Lookup Tables” with Neural Networks.

Blog Topics (Theory):

  1. The Curse of Dimensionality: Why tables fail in complex worlds (e.g., Images).
  2. Deep Q-Networks (DQN): The 2015 DeepMind breakthrough.
  3. Stability Tricks: Experience Replay Buffers and Target Networks.
  4. The “Rainbow” Improvements: Double DQN, Dueling Architectures, and Prioritized Experience Replay (PER).

Kaggle Notebooks (Practice):

  • Notebook 3: DQN on CartPole. Build a DQN using PyTorch/TensorFlow. Focus on the “Training Loop.”
  • Notebook 4: Mastering Atari (Pong/Breakout). Implementing CNN-based DQN.
  • Tip: Use CleanRL implementations here for readability, then Stable Baselines3 to show production-grade code.

Phase 3: Policy Gradients & Continuous Control (Hero)

Focus: Handling robotics and environments where actions are not just “Left/Right” but continuous values.

Blog Topics (Theory):

  1. Policy Gradients (PG): The REINFORCE algorithm and the Log-Derivative trick.
  2. The Variance Problem: Why PG is noisy and how “Baselines” fix it.
  3. Actor-Critic Methods: Combining Value (Critic) and Policy (Actor) learning (A2C/A3C).

Kaggle Notebooks (Practice):

  • Notebook 5: REINFORCE on LunarLander.
  • Notebook 6: Actor-Critic on Acrobot. Visualize how the Critic reduces the variance of the Actor.

Phase 4: State-of-the-Art Algorithms (Elite)

Focus: The algorithms actually used in the industry today (OpenAI, DeepMind).

Blog Topics (Theory):

  1. PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization): The industry standard. Explain “Clipping” and “Trust Regions” simply.
  • Visual:

  2. Off-Policy Wizards: DDPG (Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient) and TD3.
  3. SAC (Soft Actor-Critic): Adding “Entropy” to encourage exploration. This is crucial for modern robotics.

Kaggle Notebooks (Practice):

  • Notebook 7: PPO on BipedalWalker (Hard Mode).
  • Notebook 8: SAC on Humanoid (MuJoCo/PyBullet). Show how SAC explores better than PPO.

Phase 5: The Frontier (God Mode)

Focus: Generative AI, LLMs, and Offline RL. This makes your course “Elite.”

Blog Topics (Theory):

  1. Model-Based RL: DreamerV3 and World Models. Learning a model of the world to “dream” future states.
  2. Offline RL: Learning from static datasets without interacting with the environment (Conservative Q-Learning).
  3. RLHF (RL from Human Feedback): How ChatGPT uses PPO to align language models.
  4. Decision Transformers: Treating RL as a sequence modeling problem (like GPT).

Kaggle Notebooks (Practice):

  • Notebook 9: Offline RL with d3rlpy. Train an agent using only a saved dataset of replays.
  • Notebook 10: Mini-RLHF. Fine-tune a small Language Model (e.g., GPT-2 or TinyLlama) to output positive sentiments using a Reward Model and PPO (use the trl library from Hugging Face).
